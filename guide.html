🧠 What's on it?

  • Flashcards for every topic. Answer by multiple choice, gap fill or typing.
  • Quiz: quick quizzes, 9-mark practice, exam skills and full mock papers.
  • Arcade: boss battles, Psychle, Swipe Sort, Which Study? and more.
  • Weak spots shows which topics need the most work.

🎮 Why students keep coming back

  • Earn XP and climb 80 levels.
  • Keep a daily 🔥 streak and hit a daily goal.
  • Collect badges, unlock themes and hunt for secret scrolls.
  • Look after Professor Squeaks, a lab rat who grows as you revise.

📲 Install it like an app

iPhone / iPad: open the site in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Android / Chromebook / PC: open the site in Chrome, open the menu and choose Install app.

Once installed it opens full screen and works offline.

👪 For parents: test your child

Tap 👪 Parent mode at the bottom of the site. Pick the topics, read each question aloud and let your child answer in their own words. Tap Show answer to check, then tap how they did. Their progress updates automatically.

No psychology knowledge needed. Ten minutes a few times a week really helps.

💾 Back up your progress

Progress is saved in the browser on that device only. If you clear your browser, change phone or switch to a different device, it won't come with you.

Go to the 💾 Save tab and tap Copy save code or Download backup file. Keep it somewhere safe (an email to yourself works well). Paste it into the Save tab on any device to carry on where you left off. The site will remind you once a week.

⏱️ A good routine

  1. Do Today's plan at the top of the page.
  2. Clear your due reviews. Cards you got wrong come back until you know them.
  3. Try Type it for harder recall (and 2× XP).
  4. Once a week, do a 9-marker or an exam skills question.

🔒 Privacy

No accounts, no logins, no adverts. Nothing a student does on the site is sent anywhere or stored online. It all stays on their own device.
